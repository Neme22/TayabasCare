About

Ensuring Public Health & Safety

TayabasCare is committed to protecting the health and well-being of Tayabas City residents through systematic sanitary inspection and risk assessment.

🏥

Public Health Protection

Safeguarding the health of Tayabas City residents by ensuring all establishments meet sanitary standards and regulations.

📊

Data-Driven Decisions

Utilizing historical inspection data to prescribe establishment violations and identify potential health risks before they become critical.

🤝

Community Partnership

Working together with establishment owners and the community to maintain high standards of hygiene and safety.

Features

Powerful Tools for Inspection

Our system provides comprehensive tools to streamline the inspection process and ensure thorough monitoring of all establishments.

📋

Digital Sanitary Inspection Records

Replace paper-based inspections with digital records that are easy to access, search, and analyze. All inspection data is securely stored and readily available.

⚠️

Risk-Based Establishment Monitoring

Prioritize inspections based on risk levels. Our system categorizes establishments and schedules inspections accordingly for maximum efficiency.

🔐

Secure Role-Based Access

Different access levels for administrators, inspectors, and other stakeholders. Each user sees only what they need, ensuring data security.

🤖

Prescriptive Establishment Violations

Leverage historical inspection data to prescribe establishment violations, enabling proactive intervention and compliance enforcement.

Process

How It Works

Get started with TayabasCare in three simple steps. Our streamlined process makes inspections efficient and effective.

01

Login to the System

Access the system with your credentials. Different roles provide different levels of access and functionality.

02

Select Establishment

Choose the establishment you want to inspect from the database. View history and risk assessment data.

03

Complete Inspection

Conduct the inspection using our digital checklist. Generate reports and submit findings instantly.
